Stock controllersBETA

Xiaomi / Ninebot (beta, read only): speed, battery %, trip, odometer, temperature and error codes. Works on older Xiaomi and Segway-Ninebot firmware. Newer firmware is encrypted and won't send data. Gears and the speed limit only work with VESC.

ScannerBETA

For Kukirin, Dualtron, Boyueda and other stock controllers. Their Bluetooth language isn't public, so the scanner connects to any scooter and records what it sends. Turn the scooter on, scan, ride a bit, change gears and lights, then tap Copy log and send it to me so I can add support.

Gears

  

  

  

  

  
The speed limit on the dashboard is sent when you tap Apply. Follow your local speed laws on public roads. With "Gears change the controller" on, gear and single/dual changes set temporary limits on the VESC. They reset when the controller restarts. Only use it on a VESC you've already set up and tested.

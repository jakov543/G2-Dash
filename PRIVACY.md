# G2 Dash privacy policy

**Without an account:** G2 Dash stores your settings, rides and odometer only on your device.
Nothing is sent to any server.

**With an account (optional):** we store your email address and a copy of your app data
(settings, ride history, odometer) on our account server (Supabase) so you can sync between devices.
We don't sell or share this data and there are no ads or tracking.

- Bluetooth and GPS data are used only inside the app to show your dashboard.
- Location is never uploaded. Only ride totals (distance, time, speed) are synced.

## Deleting your account
In the app open **Settings → Account → Delete my account** (or **Settings → Delete account**).
This permanently deletes your account, your email and all synced data from our server and
removes the data from your device.

**Settings → Delete all data on this device** removes local data only.
